The Invitational

More than a tournament.

The DeWees Invitational has never been defined by one game. It has grown from cards, to football and poker, to golf and poker—changing with the years while preserving the reason it began.

The heart of the Invitational is the annual gathering itself: a group of friends, genuine competition, plenty of laughter, and stories that become part of the next year’s event.

How it grew

One event.
Several eras.

01
The Early Years

Cards, competition and an idea taking shape

The Invitational began as an annual event centered on friends and cards. DI III in 2008 introduced the first formal poker tournament, won by Mike Herman. By the fifth annual event, “The Bracelet Year” had already created a piece of history worth preserving.

02
The Football Years

Football by day. Poker by night.

For many years, the day developed its familiar rhythm: football, food, then the poker tournament.

03
The Golf Years

A new format, the same annual tradition

Golf replaced football, bringing a welcome new chapter without changing the purpose of the Invitational.
